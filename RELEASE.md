**iPhone with iOS 17 or later.** Download **NotoNotes-unsigned.ipa** from Assets to your computer. This package needs to be signed for your phone before iOS will open it.

For personal installation, open [MobAI Desktop](https://mobai.run/download), connect and unlock your iPhone over USB, trust the computer, and use MobAI to install the IPA with Apple Account re-signing. A free Apple Account can be used. [MobAI installation guide](https://github.com/MobAI-App/ios-builder#install-on-a-device)

A Safari download alone cannot install this unsigned package. A public TestFlight invitation or App Store listing will be needed for ordinary phone-only installation.

Notes, search, and calendar work offline. Optional cloud writing requires a configured backend.
